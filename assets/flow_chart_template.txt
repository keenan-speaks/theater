START
 |
 v
Initialize seating chart (all seats available)
 |
 v
Set ticket price ($12.50)
 |
 v
Set choice = 'Y'
 |
 v
WHILE (choice == 'Y' or 'y')
 |
 v
  Display seating chart
 |
 v
  Prompt user for row and seat
 |
 v
  Validate row (1-15) and seat (1-30)
  |
  +--- [INVALID] ---> Display error message, clear input, re-prompt
  |
  +--- [VALID] --->
            |
            v
        IF seat available
            |
            +--- [YES] ---> Reserve seat, Display "Seat reserved"
            |
            +--- [NO] ----> Display "Seat already taken"
            |
            v
        Calculate and display current sales
            |
            v
        Ask if user wants to reserve another seat (Y/N)
            |
            v
        Read choice
 |
 v
[Exit WHILE loop]
 |
 v
Display "Thank You! Final Sales: $..."
 |
 v
Display Final Seating Chart
 |
 v
END
